联系我们

使用中遇到问题、发现内容错误，或有功能建议，欢迎发邮件至 rudykobe@outlook.com。我们通常在 2 个工作日内回复。为便于定位问题，请附上：设备型号、iOS 版本、App 版本，以及问题截图（如方便）。

常见问题

1. 为什么同一天的卦象不会变化？

本应用的每日卦象以当地日期的子时为界生成，全天保持一致，方便你在一天中反复回看同一卦。次日子时会更新为新的卦象。

2. 我的出生信息会被上传吗？

不会。出生信息、命盘与学习进度都只保存在你的设备本地，应用运行时不向服务器发送数据。卸载应用会一并删除这些数据。

3. 换手机后如何恢复？

永久会员可通过付费页的「恢复购买」找回权益（需使用购买时的同一 Apple ID）。出生信息与学习进度不做云端同步，需要在新设备上重新输入。

4. 如何取消订阅或申请退款？

订阅与退款均由 Apple 管理：打开「设置 → Apple ID → 订阅」可管理或取消；退款请前往 Apple 的「报告问题」页面申请。我们无法代为操作。

5. 如何删除我的数据？

在应用内删除命盘，或直接卸载应用，即可清除本机全部数据。

6. 解读内容可以作为决策依据吗？

不可以。卦象与命理解读为传统文献释义，仅供文化研习与自我参考，不构成医疗、法律、投资建议，也不承诺任何结果。

7. 应用闪退或页面空白怎么办？

请先确认已更新到最新版本并重启设备；若仍存在问题，请邮件告知我们机型、系统版本与出现问题的步骤，我们会尽快排查。

Contact

For issues, content corrections, or feature requests, email rudykobe@outlook.com. We usually reply within two business days. Please include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a screenshot if possible.

FAQ

1. Why does the daily hexagram stay the same all day?

The daily hexagram is generated at the start of the local day and stays fixed until the next day, so you can revisit the same reading. It refreshes at midnight.

2. Is my birth information uploaded?

No. Birth details, charts, and progress are stored only on your device. The app sends nothing to a server. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

3. How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Use “Restore Purchases” on the paywall with the same Apple ID you bought with. Birth details and progress are not cloud-synced, so you will need to enter them again.

4. How do I cancel a subscription or request a refund?

Subscriptions and refunds are handled by Apple. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions; request refunds via Apple’s “Report a Problem” page. We cannot do this on your behalf.

5. How do I delete my data?

Delete your chart in the app, or uninstall the app, to remove all local data.

6. Can readings be used to make decisions?

No. Readings are interpretations of traditional texts for cultural study and reflection. They are not medical, legal, or financial advice, and no outcome is promised.

7. The app crashes or shows a blank page.

Please update to the latest version and restart your device. If the problem persists, email us your device model, OS version, and the steps that trigger it.

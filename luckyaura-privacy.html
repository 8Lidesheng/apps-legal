中文版

LuckyAura（幸运光环，以下简称“本应用”）是一款《易经》与八字传统文化研习工具。我们尽量少地处理你的信息，本政策说明具体情况。

一、我们处理的信息

  1. 你输入的出生信息：出生日期与出生时间，用于在本机排盘并生成每日卦象与解读。该信息仅保存在你的设备本地，不会上传到任何服务器，我们也无法访问。
  2. 使用记录：收藏、课程学习进度、免责声明确认状态等，仅保存在本机。
  3. 购买信息：应用内购买由 Apple 通过 App Store 完成。我们不会接触或存储你的姓名、银行卡号、账单地址等支付信息；购买状态由 Apple StoreKit 在本机校验。

二、设备权限

本应用不请求定位、通讯录、相册、相机、麦克风、健康等系统隐私权限，不使用广告标识符（IDFA），不进行跨应用追踪。

三、第三方服务

本应用仅使用 Apple 提供的系统能力（StoreKit 内购）。未接入任何第三方广告、统计、推送或数据分析 SDK。

四、网络与数据传输

卦象、课程与解读内容均内置于安装包中，应用运行时不向我们的服务器发送任何数据。我们不会出售、出租或共享你的个人信息。

五、存储与删除

全部数据保存在你的设备本地。卸载本应用即删除本机数据，且无法恢复。App Store 订阅关系由 Apple 管理。

六、未成年人

本应用为传统文化学习与自省工具，并非面向儿童设计。未成年人应在监护人指导下使用，发生内购前应取得监护人同意。

七、免责说明

卦象与命理解读为传统文献释义，仅供文化研习与自我参考，不构成医疗、法律、投资或任何专业建议，也不承诺任何结果或效果。

八、政策更新

我们可能适时更新本政策。更新后的版本将发布在本页面，并更新页面顶部的日期。

九、联系我们

如对本政策有任何疑问，可通过邮箱 rudykobe@outlook.com 与我们联系。

English Version

LuckyAura is a study tool for the I Ching (Book of Changes) and the Four Pillars tradition. This policy explains the limited information the app handles.

1. Information We Handle

  • Birth information you enter: your date and time of birth, used on your device to build a chart and generate daily readings. It stays in local storage on your device and is never uploaded; we cannot access it.
  • Usage records: favorites, course progress, and your acknowledgement of the disclaimer. These are stored locally only.
  • Purchase information: in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your name, card number, or billing address; purchase status is verified locally through StoreKit.

2. Device Permissions

The app does not request location, contacts, photo library, camera, microphone, or health permissions. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and performs no cross-app tracking.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses only Apple system frameworks (StoreKit for purchases). It contains no third-party advertising, analytics, push, or data SDKs.

4. Network and Data Transfer

All hexagram, course, and reading content is bundled in the app. The app sends no data to our servers. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information.

5. Storage and Deletion

All data lives on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently. App Store subscriptions are managed by Apple.

6. Minors

The app is a cultural study and reflection tool and is not designed for children. Minors should use it under guardian supervision, and any purchase requires guardian consent.

7. Disclaimer

Readings are interpretations of traditional texts, provided for cultural study and personal reflection only. They are not medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice, and no outcome is promised.

8. Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page with a new date.

9. Contact

Questions? Email us at rudykobe@outlook.com.

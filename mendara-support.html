常见问题

1. 提醒没有按时出现

请在「设置 → 通知 → Mendara」中确认通知已允许，并检查是否开启了专注模式或低电量模式。提醒由系统本地调度，重新打开应用一次可帮助恢复调度。

2. 是否必须登录？

不需要。使用「通过 Apple 登录」是可选项，仅用于在设备上保留一个身份标识；不登录也能使用全部记录与计划功能。

3. 我的记录会上传吗？

不会。打卡、节律与设置均保存在设备本地，账号信息保存在钥匙串。应用运行时不向服务器发送数据。

4. 如何恢复购买？

在订阅页点击「恢复购买」，并使用购买时的同一 Apple ID。若仍无法恢复，请邮件提供购买日期与订单号后四位。

5. 如何取消订阅？

订阅由 Apple 管理：打开「设置 → Apple ID → 订阅」即可取消或切换。退款请通过 Apple「报告问题」页面申请。

6. 如何删除我的数据？

在设置中退出登录可清除账号标识；卸载应用会删除全部本地记录与设置，且无法恢复。

7. 应用提供的建议可以替代医生吗？

不可以。本应用是生活方式与自我管理工具，不是医疗器械，不提供诊断或治疗建议。如有健康问题，请咨询专业医疗人员。

FAQ

1. My reminder did not appear on time

Check that notifications are allowed in Settings → Notifications → Mendara, and that Focus or Low Power Mode is not blocking delivery. Reminders are scheduled locally; opening the app once can help re-establish scheduling.

2. Do I have to sign in?

No. Sign in with Apple is optional and only keeps an identity on your device. All recording and planning features work without an account.

3. Are my records uploaded?

No. Check-ins, rhythm entries, and settings stay on your device; account information stays in the Keychain. The app sends no data to a server.

4. How do I restore my purchase?

Tap “Restore Purchases” on the subscription screen with the same Apple ID used to buy. If it still fails, email us the purchase date and the last four digits of the order number.

5. How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions to cancel or change. Refunds are requested through Apple’s “Report a Problem” page.

6. How do I delete my data?

Signing out in Settings clears the stored account identifier. Uninstalling the app deletes all local records and settings permanently.

7. Can the app’s suggestions replace a doctor?

No. Mendara is a lifestyle and self-management tool, not a medical device, and provides no diagnosis or treatment advice. Please consult a qualified medical professional for health concerns.

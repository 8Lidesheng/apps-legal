中文版

手可摘星辰（以下简称“本应用”）是一款公职类考试刷题工具。我们按最小必要原则处理信息，本政策说明具体情况。

一、我们处理的信息

  1. 学习记录：你的做题记录、错题本、学习进度与收藏的地区，仅保存在你的设备本地（iOS 本地存储），用于继续练习与错题回顾。
  2. 购买信息：会员订阅由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。我们不会接触或存储你的姓名、银行卡号、账单地址等支付信息；订阅状态由 Apple StoreKit 在本机校验。

本应用不需要注册或登录，我们不收集手机号、邮箱、身份信息或位置信息。

二、设备权限

本应用不请求定位、通讯录、相册、相机、麦克风、通知等系统权限，不使用广告标识符（IDFA），不进行跨应用追踪。

三、第三方服务

本应用仅使用 Apple 提供的系统能力（StoreKit 内购）。未接入第三方广告、统计或数据分析 SDK。

四、网络与数据传输

题库与解析内置于安装包，可离线使用，应用运行时不向我们的服务器发送数据。我们不会出售、出租或共享你的个人信息。

五、存储与删除

学习记录保存在本机，卸载应用即删除且无法恢复。App Store 订阅关系由 Apple 管理。

六、内容来源与免责

题目与解析来源于公开渠道，仅供学习参考。本应用与任何政府机关、考试主办方均无隶属或合作关系，不代表官方立场，也不对考试结果作出任何承诺。

七、未成年人

未成年人应在监护人指导下使用；发生内购前应取得监护人同意。

八、政策更新

我们可能适时更新本政策，更新后的版本将发布在本页面并更新日期。

九、联系我们

如对本政策有任何疑问，可通过邮箱 rudykobe@outlook.com 与我们联系。

English Version

Shou Ke Zhai Xing Chen (“the app”) is a practice-question tool for public-sector exams in China. We handle information on a minimal, necessary basis.

1. Information We Handle

  • Study records: practice history, wrong-answer notebook, progress, and favorited regions are stored only in local storage on your device, so you can resume practice and review mistakes.
  • Purchase information: membership subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your name, card number, or billing address; subscription status is verified locally through StoreKit.

The app requires no registration or sign-in. We do not collect phone numbers, email addresses, identity documents, or location data.

2. Device Permissions

The app does not request location, contacts, photo library, camera, microphone, or notification permissions. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and performs no cross-app tracking.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses only Apple system frameworks (StoreKit for purchases). It contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or data SDKs.

4. Network and Data Transfer

Question banks and explanations are bundled in the app and work offline. The app sends no data to our servers. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information.

5. Storage and Deletion

Study records are stored on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them permanently. App Store subscriptions are managed by Apple.

6. Content Source and Disclaimer

Questions and explanations are compiled from publicly available sources for study reference only. The app is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any government body or exam organizer, does not represent official positions, and makes no promise about exam results.

7. Minors

Minors should use the app under guardian supervision, and any purchase requires guardian consent.

8. Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page with a new date.

9. Contact

Questions? Email us at rudykobe@outlook.com.

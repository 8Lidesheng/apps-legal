中文版

Mendara（骑马，以下简称“本应用”）是一款帮助你记录身体节律、建立日常习惯并放松身心的个人工具。本政策说明我们如何处理你的信息。

一、我们处理的信息

  1. 可选账号：你可以选择使用「通过 Apple 登录」。Apple 会返回一个用户标识，以及你同意共享的姓名。这些内容仅保存在你设备的钥匙串中；我们不交换授权码、不建立服务器账号，也不会上传。
  2. 习惯与节律记录：每日打卡、节律记录、计划与进度，仅保存在设备本地。本应用不读取 Apple 健康（HealthKit）数据。
  3. 提醒设置：如你开启提醒，通知由系统在设备本地定时触发，内容不经过网络。
  4. 购买信息：订阅与内购由 Apple 处理，我们不接触你的支付卡号或账单信息；购买状态通过 StoreKit 在本机校验。

二、设备权限

本应用可能请求：通知权限（用于本地提醒，可随时关闭）、通过 Apple 登录（完全可选）。不请求定位、通讯录、相册、相机、麦克风或健康数据权限，不使用广告标识符（IDFA），不进行跨应用追踪。

三、第三方服务

本应用仅使用 Apple 提供的系统能力（StoreKit、通知、通过 Apple 登录）。未接入第三方广告、统计或数据分析 SDK。

四、网络与数据传输

应用运行时不向我们的服务器发送数据。我们不会出售、出租或共享你的个人信息。

五、存储与删除

记录与设置保存在设备本地，账号信息保存在钥匙串。你可以在设置中退出登录，或卸载应用以删除全部本地数据。App Store 订阅关系由 Apple 管理。

六、未成年人

本应用面向一般用户，并非为儿童设计。未成年人应在监护人指导下使用。

七、健康免责

本应用是生活方式与自我管理工具，不是医疗器械，不提供诊断、治疗或用药建议。如有健康问题，请咨询专业医疗人员。

八、政策更新

我们可能适时更新本政策，更新后的版本将发布在本页面并更新日期。

九、联系我们

如对本政策有任何疑问，可通过邮箱 rudykobe@outlook.com 与我们联系。

English Version

Mendara is a personal tool for recording your daily rhythm, building habits, and winding down. This policy explains how your information is handled.

1. Information We Handle

  • Optional account: you may sign in with Apple. Apple returns a user identifier and any name you agree to share. This is stored only in the Keychain on your device. We do not exchange the authorization code, create a server-side account, or upload it.
  • Habit and rhythm records: daily check-ins, rhythm entries, plans, and progress are stored locally on your device. The app does not read Apple Health (HealthKit) data.
  • Reminders: if enabled, notifications are scheduled and delivered locally by the system; their content does not travel over the network.
  • Purchase information: subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your card or billing details; purchase status is verified locally through StoreKit.

2. Device Permissions

The app may request notification permission (for local reminders, which you can disable at any time) and Sign in with Apple (entirely optional). It does not request location, contacts, photo library, camera, microphone, or health permissions. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and performs no cross-app tracking.

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses only Apple system frameworks (StoreKit, local notifications, Sign in with Apple). It contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or data SDKs.

4. Network and Data Transfer

The app sends no data to our servers. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information.

5. Storage and Deletion

Records and settings are stored locally; account information is stored in the Keychain. You can sign out in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove all local data. App Store subscriptions are managed by Apple.

6. Minors

The app is intended for a general audience and is not designed for children. Minors should use it under guardian supervision.

7. Health Disclaimer

Mendara is a lifestyle and self-management tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or medication advice. If you have a health concern, please consult a qualified medical professional.

8. Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page with a new date.

9. Contact

Questions? Email us at rudykobe@outlook.com.

联系我们

题目纠错、功能建议或使用问题，欢迎发邮件至 rudykobe@outlook.com。请尽量附上：题目所在模块、题干截图（或题干首句）、设备型号与系统版本。我们通常在 2 个工作日内回复。

常见问题

1. 题库来自哪里？会更新吗？

题目与解析来源于公开渠道，按模块整理，并随应用版本更新。发现答案有误时，欢迎邮件指出，我们会核实并修正。

2. 可以离线做题吗？

可以。题库内置于应用，无需联网即可练习；首次进入某个模块时如有加载提示，稍等即可，之后可正常离线使用。

3. 换手机后记录和会员怎么办？

会员可在会员页点击「恢复购买」，使用购买时的同一 Apple ID 找回。做题记录与错题本仅保存在本机，不跨设备同步，换机后需要重新积累。

4. 如何取消订阅？

订阅由 Apple 管理：打开「设置 → Apple ID → 订阅」即可取消或切换。退款请通过 Apple 的「报告问题」页面申请，我们无法代为操作。

5. 如何清空做题记录？

可在应用的记录/设置页面清空错题本与进度；卸载应用会删除全部本地数据且无法恢复。

6. 应用里的题目是官方题库吗？

不是。本应用与任何政府机关、考试主办方均无隶属或合作关系，题目来自公开渠道，仅供学习参考，不代表官方答案。

7. 应用打不开或闪退怎么办？

请先更新到最新版本并重启设备；若问题仍在，请邮件告知机型、系统版本和复现步骤。

Contact

For question corrections, feature requests, or troubleshooting, email rudykobe@outlook.com. Please include the module, a screenshot or the first line of the question, your device model, and OS version. We usually reply within two business days.

FAQ

1. Where do the questions come from? Are they updated?

Questions and explanations are compiled from publicly available sources, organized by module, and updated with app releases. If you find an error, email us and we will verify and correct it.

2. Can I practice offline?

Yes. The question bank is bundled in the app and works without a network connection.

3. How do I keep my membership and records on a new phone?

Tap “Restore Purchases” on the membership screen with the same Apple ID used to buy. Practice records and the wrong-answer notebook are stored only on the device and are not synced across devices.

4. How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions to cancel or change. Refunds are requested through Apple’s “Report a Problem” page.

5. How do I clear my practice records?

Clear the wrong-answer notebook and progress on the records/settings screen. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data permanently.

6. Are these official exam questions?

No. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government body or exam organizer. Questions come from public sources for study reference only and do not represent official answers.

7. The app won’t open or keeps crashing.

Update to the latest version and restart your device. If the problem persists, email us your device model, OS version, and reproduction steps.
